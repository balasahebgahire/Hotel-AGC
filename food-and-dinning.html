Viceroy Restaurant (Pure Veg)

Multi cusine restaurant serving indian, chinese, local cusine

Viceroy restaurant is situated on the ground floor. It seats 60 people at a time. We serve buffet breakfast from 07am to 10:00am, consisting of Indian, Chinese, Continental and local dishes along with traditional breakfast items like fresh juices, cornflakes, toast with butter and preserves. For lunch and dinner we have a ala cart menu to choose from. For people who want light meals we serve pizzas, burgers, sandwiches and other local snacks. Our chefs are professional in vegetarian dishes. Our waiting staff are trained to provide personalized service to our guests.

Tandoor or Indian oven

We specialise in tandoori items like tandoori platter which consists of different types of grilled kababs and indian bread like naan.

  • Sumptuous Indian and chinese meals
  • Vegetables are procured from the market daily.
  • Bed tea, breakfast, lunch, high tea, soup, dinner,
  • Well-trained staff to attend to your needs

Our Specialities

Our restaurant Viceroy, serves delicious vegetarian variety of indian, chinese and fast food.

Corriander Shorba

Corriander shorba is a extremely popular product of our kitchen. This soup is made from fresh corriander leaves and mild aromatic spices.

Tandoori Platter

This is a combination of several vegetable kebas grilled in an indian oven served on an hot plate.

Methi Mutter Malai

Methi mutter malai is a super-hit combination of fenugreek and fresh green peas with mild spices.

Baingan Maratha

Baigan maratha is a favourite local speciality made of small brinjals and spicy black spices.
